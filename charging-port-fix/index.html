Why You Should NEVER Put Your Phone in Rice

Important information regarding this tool

When you plug in your phone and see the dreaded "Liquid Detected in Lightning Connector" or "Moisture Detected" warning, your first instinct might be to panic and bury it in a bag of rice. Please don't do this!

Rice dust and starch can easily get inside the wet charging port. When the water mixes with the starch, it turns into a glue-like paste that hardens over the charging pins, permanently ruining your port. The only safe way to dry it is by letting it air dry in a well-ventilated space. While you wait, you can explore other useful utilities on DevDesk App.
