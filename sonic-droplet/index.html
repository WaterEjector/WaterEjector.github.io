Frequently Asked Questions

How does Ghost Replay racing work?

When you complete a run, tap Challenge Friend to generate an encoded link. When your friend opens the link, a translucent ghost of your droplet will fly beside them in real time!

Can I control the droplet using my voice or microphone?

Yes! Tap the microphone icon on the top bar. You can blow or vocalize into your device microphone to trigger acoustic pulse jumps hands-free.

Does playing this game expel water from my device?

No. While this game uses synthesized Web Audio tones inspired by our tools, it is purely intended for casual gaming entertainment. If your phone was submerged, visit our Water Eject tool to run calibrated 165Hz ejection.
