Diagnosing Microphone Damage After a Drop in Water

Important information regarding this tool

The microphone hole at the bottom of your smartphone is incredibly small. Because of this, water surface tension can easily trap a single drop of water inside the hole, blocking the acoustic mesh completely.

If you've recently dropped your phone in the sink or pool, and people say you sound like you're underwater on phone calls, your mic is likely blocked. Use our quick recording tool above to hear exactly what the person on the other end of the call is hearing. If it sounds muffled, try our Water Ejector to push the water out.
