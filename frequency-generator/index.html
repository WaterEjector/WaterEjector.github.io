The Power of Manual Sound Frequencies

Important information regarding this tool

While our standard Water Eject mode uses a highly optimized 165Hz pulsed tone, every smartphone and speaker has a slightly different physical design. Some smaller devices might need a higher pitched sound (like 300Hz or 440Hz) to properly shake the water out.

Use this manual frequency generator to find the perfect "sweet spot" for your device. Just slide the bar until you feel the strongest vibration. If you're a developer or audio tester, this tool is also great for checking speaker frequency response. (Brought to you by DevDesk App).
