Why Test Your Stereo Balance?

Important information regarding this tool

After dropping your phone or earbuds in water, it's very common for only one side to become muffled or quiet. Sometimes it's water damage, and other times it's just earwax buildup blocking the speaker grill.

By using this tool to play sound exclusively in your Left or Right channel, you can easily figure out which speaker is struggling. If you find out that one speaker is definitely blocked, you can head back to our main Water Eject tool to blast the water out, or check out our sponsor DevDesk App for more awesome tech tools.
